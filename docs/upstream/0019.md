# Preserve TextView selection through opt-in layout controls

A Reader with draggable side panels loses its document selection twice: the
window selection layer clears it on the splitter's MouseDown, then TextView
clears geometric selection when its width changes. Reseeding selection after
the drag masks both failures.

Patch0019 adds per-frame opt-in control hit regions to TextSelection. Pointer
gestures beginning in those regions leave selection alone without consuming
control input. Removed controls leave no registered area, and a lost MouseUp
cannot exempt a later ordinary document press. Ordinary document/search clicks
keep their existing behavior. An event-predicate overload lets callers reuse
their exact clipped glyph hit testing and opt in only a single click; the
bounds wrapper keeps its existing semantics.

TextViewState also gains an opt-in `retain_selection_on_layout` builder. A
settled selection retains its inline byte ranges instead of recalculating them
from old pixel coordinates after reflow. Stable top-level block keys retain
fully enclosed virtual blocks. Content revisions still clear selection.
MouseUp capture materializes the final ordinary drag against the painted layout
before the window controller settles it, even when final Move and Up arrive
before a draw. Boundary ranges are retained only for matching captured
participant-relative endpoints and parsed-content revision. Per-frame window origin and scroll
projection are excluded from that identity, so reflow does not reproject old
content pixels into different wrapping. SelectAll takes precedence over retained partial ranges. Defaults remain unchanged; GPUI core
is untouched.

Tessera's focused widget regression creates a real partial text selection before
MouseDown/drag/MouseUp on both native splitters, then clicks panel toggle/Close
controls and checks the same selected content. It also checks viewport changes,
removed-control geometry and ordinary document clicks, including a lost release.
The toolkit regression batches real input dispatch before effects/drawing and
asserts final selection inside that batch. It also checks partial-to-SelectAll
materialized highlight ranges independently from the copy shortcut. Live
splitter edge probes cover the pinned 8px hitbox, including adjacent outside
presses. A unique partial selection on a later wrapped line survives actual
Notes resize, toggle and scroll after registration-triggered frames settle; new
ordinary gestures and document replacement still invalidate it.
The patch applies with the link-presentation patch0018 and parse-status patch0020.
