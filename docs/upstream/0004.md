# text_selection: drag-autoscroll stalled at the padded reader edge

The confirmed field cause is the reader's padding, not pointer capture. In the
clipped row containing a `scrollable(true)` TextView with `py_3()`, the inner
`List` ends 12 px above the clipping edge. The synthetic wheel position is
clamped inside the content mask, but that position can fall in the padding below
the List, outside its hitbox. No list handler consumes the wheel there.

The synthetic wheel does scroll a virtualized list when its position hits that
list. The [maintainer's measurement](https://github.com/longbridge/gpui-kit/pull/2948#issuecomment-5540782842)
and the [follow-up reproduction](https://github.com/longbridge/gpui-kit/pull/2948#issuecomment-5551008307)
established this distinction. The original claim that pointer capture prevented
the list from handling synthetic scroll events was incorrect.

In the padded harness on upstream `main` at `928c3eb7`, the List ends at y=288
and the content mask at y=300. Over four ticks, a pointer at y=286 inside the
List moves it 52.33 px; y=298 in the padding moves it 0 px.

## Reproduction

Open a long document in the clipped, padded reader layout described above.
Anchor a selection near the top of the viewport, drag into the padding at the
bottom edge and hold with small movements for several seconds.

Historical field measurement before the rework: 726 events with
`delta = Some(16.33 px)` were dispatched while `px_off` stayed `0.0`; selection
captured only the visible viewport (≈ 2 KB of a 132 KB note). This records the
original symptom, not evidence for the rejected pointer-capture explanation.

## Fix

Reworked after review (fork branch `fix/autoscroll-notify-participant` @ `bee77ea`
on `928c3eb7`; [rework notes](https://github.com/longbridge/gpui-kit/pull/2948#issuecomment-5551008307)).
The first revision notified the participant *and* kept the synthetic wheel, which
double-scrolled a self-scrolling `TextView` (156.67 px in a 4-tick window bounded
at 78.33 px). Now `TextSelectionRegistration` carries a `self_scroll` flag that
`TextView` sets from `scrollable`; `update_auto_scroll` notifies a self-scrolling
participant (measured against the registration's own bounds, via
`update_participant_auto_scroll`) and returns, and the synthetic wheel runs only
for participants without the flag. `update_auto_scroll` takes `&Window`, the
`None` arm is gone, and the anchor lookup is shared by the three call sites.

The field repro is the clipped, padded reader: the `List` inside a `py_3()`
`TextView` ends 12 px above the row's bottom edge, the wheel is clamped into the
padding band where no hitbox handles it, so a drag held at the pane edge moved
0 px. New harness test `padded_reader_text_view_auto_scrolls_at_the_clipping_edge`
builds that layout and fails on `main`; the existing both-directions test now
asserts `0 < moved <= ticks * per_tick`. `crates/base/src/text/{selection_adapter,
text_view}.rs`, `crates/base/src/text_selection.rs`,
`crates/component/src/text/window_selection.rs`, +191 −69, no gpui core change.
