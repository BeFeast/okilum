# Prepare a workshop visit kit

This is synthetic acceptance input, not an already-created canonical goal. Copy
only the two files under `sources/` into a fresh test brain. Create the actual goal
through Okilum so the backend allocates and retains its identities and journal.

## Initial inbox thought

Prepare a packing and shopping checklist for the sample workshop visit. Use the
workshop brief and current supplies in this brain, work out what is missing, and
save a short checklist with citations to both notes. Include the shopping deadline,
departure time and spending limit. This assignment produces a plan only.

## Conversation constraint

Do not buy supplies, send messages, edit the source notes or mark physical errands
as done. The outcome is a sourced Markdown plan returned automatically from T3.

## Goal criterion to record before dispatch

- Procedure label: `C1` (retain the actual ID generated by the UI)
- Description: A reviewer has opened the returned Markdown checklist and verified
  its required quantities, stock shortages, purchasing cost, deadline, departure
  time and citations against both source notes.
- `requires_human`: `true`

Explicit review is intentional for this fixture: it exercises the attention and
human-evidence path without accepting a provider's success label as verification.
It does not impose human review on every product goal. The backend must preserve
the returned result before review and keep this criterion unmet until review occurs.
After independently reviewing the saved artifact against the oracle, record
`accept_human` with the actual reviewer/time and reviewed artifact reference.
Its canonical human receipt satisfies this human criterion. `criterion_evaluate`
applies to nonhuman criteria and is not used for this fixture's C1.
